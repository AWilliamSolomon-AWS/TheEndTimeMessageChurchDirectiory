THE END TIME MESSAGE CHURCH DIRECTORY

About The Directory

A digital platform established to connect believers and End Time Message assemblies worldwide for the honour and glory of the Lord Jesus Christ.

Vision

To establish a worldwide digital platform that connects End Time Message churches across nations, making it easier for believers to discover places of worship, fellowship and spiritual encouragement. A vision to bring churches closer through technology, overcome geographical boundaries and create a connected global Message community, all for the honour and glory of the Lord Jesus Christ.

Mission

To make End Time Message church information accessible, organized and available to people around the world. Through a unified digital platform, the mission is to help believers discover churches, explore church locations, access relevant information and connect with local assemblies. To use modern technology as a meaningful instrument in serving the Christian community and making church information accessible across nations.

Purpose

To create a reliable, accessible and comprehensive worldwide directory of End Time Message churches. Whether a believer is travelling to another country, relocating to a new city or searching for a nearby Message church, the purpose is to make that discovery simple and convenient. To build a valuable digital resource that promotes connectivity, visibility and accessibility among Message churches, while keeping the Lord Jesus Christ at the centre of its purpose.
